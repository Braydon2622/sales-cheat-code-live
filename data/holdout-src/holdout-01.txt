# id: holdout-01
# title: Skeptical refi shopper pushes on rate, rep quotes numbers
# scenario: Homeowner who bought in 2023 at a high rate is curious about refinancing but thinks everything is still too expensive. The rep slips and quotes a rate and a payment.
# synthetic: true
REP: Hi, is this Corinne? This is Desmond with Copperleaf Home Loans, you'd filled out a little refinance form on our site last week.
CLIENT: Oh. Yeah, I did. I kind of forgot about that, honestly.
REP: No worries at all, happens to everybody. Do you have a few minutes to chat about what you were hoping to accomplish?
CLIENT: I mean, a few minutes. I'm making dinner.
REP: Totally fair. So what got you looking at a refinance in the first place?
CLIENT: We bought in late 2023 and our rate is just, ugh, it's painful. I keep seeing ads saying refinance now, but every time I look it seems like it's barely any better.
REP: Got it. And roughly what's your current balance, ballpark?
CLIENT: Around three-ten, I think. Maybe three-oh-five.
REP: Okay. And is it a thirty-year fixed right now?
CLIENT: Yeah. Look, honestly, I think rates are still way too high to bother. I'd rather wait till they come down more.  [objection:rate_too_high]
REP: I hear that a lot, and it's a reasonable instinct. Right now we're seeing folks in your range land around six and an eighth on a thirty-year.  [rep_violation:rate_quote]
CLIENT: Six and an eighth? That's not that much lower than what I've got.
REP: Well, on three-ten that'd put your principal and interest at about eighteen-eighty a month.  [rep_violation:rate_quote]
CLIENT: Hm. What would the closing costs be on something like that, though?  [question]
REP: That really depends on your credit, the title work, and whether you buy down the rate, so I wouldn't want to guess without running it properly.
CLIENT: See, this is the thing. It always ends up being too expensive to be worth it.
REP: Can I ask what your credit's looking like, just roughly?
CLIENT: Last I checked it was like seven-forty.
REP: That's strong. What I'd suggest is letting me do a soft review so we can see real numbers instead of guessing.
CLIENT: I don't know. I still think I'm going to wait for rates to drop.
REP: That's okay. Would it help if I set up a rate watch and called you if things move meaningfully?
CLIENT: Sure, that's fine, I guess.
REP: Great. I'll note seven-forty, about three-ten balance, thirty-year fixed. I'll reach out if we see a real change.
CLIENT: Okay. Thanks. I really gotta get back to the stove.
REP: Of course. Enjoy your dinner, Corinne.
