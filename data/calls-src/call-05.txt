# id: call-05
# title: Comparison shopper gets a rate quoted to keep her on the line
# scenario: Renee is comparing three lenders for a purchase and says she's just collecting numbers. The rep panics and quotes a specific rate to keep her interested.
# synthetic: true
REP: Hi Renee, Derek with Copperleaf Home Loans. Thanks for picking up.
CLIENT: Hi. Yeah, so, full disclosure, I'm talking to like three lenders. I'm just comparing right now.  [objection:just_shopping]
REP: Smart, you should compare. What are you buying, if you don't mind me asking?
CLIENT: A townhouse, it's a new build. They say it'll be done in the spring.
REP: Oh nice, new construction. Are you working with the builder's lender too?
CLIENT: They pushed theirs, yeah. That's one of the three.
REP: Okay. And what's most important to you in picking one, rate, fees, service?
CLIENT: Honestly, rate. That's all anyone talks about. So what's your rate today?  [question]
REP: For a townhouse purchase like that, I can get you six and an eighth today, easy.  [rep_violation:rate_quote]
CLIENT: Huh. The builder's guy said something a little higher than that.
REP: Yeah, we're usually pretty sharp.
CLIENT: Does that include points or whatever?
REP: Well, it depends on the full scenario, credit and down payment and all that.
CLIENT: Okay, so it's not really six and an eighth then.
REP: It could be. I'd need to run your full picture to say for sure.
CLIENT: Right. Okay. I've got a meeting in a few, so I'm going to have to go.
REP: Totally. Can I send you a quick application link?
CLIENT: Sure, send it over, I'll look at it.  [objection:stall]
REP: Will do. Thanks Renee.
CLIENT: Bye.
