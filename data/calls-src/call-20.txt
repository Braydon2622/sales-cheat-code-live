# id: call-20
# title: Borrower with a 640 score fishes for approval
# scenario: Tanya is a single mom with a 640 credit score hoping to buy a starter home. She asks directly whether she'll be approved and what her payment would be; the rep guarantees approval and quotes a payment.
# synthetic: true
REP: Hi Tanya, Kyle with Copperleaf Home Loans. Is this still a good time?
CLIENT: Yeah, the kids are at practice, so I've got like twenty minutes.
REP: Perfect. What sport?
CLIENT: Soccer, both of them. I spend my whole life at that field.
REP: Ha, I know the feeling. So you're looking at starter homes?
CLIENT: Yeah, something small, two or three bedrooms, around two-fifty. I'm tired of renting.
REP: That makes sense. Can I ask where your credit is right now?
CLIENT: That's the thing, it's like a six-forty. Will I get approved with a six-forty?  [question]
REP: Oh yeah, I guarantee you'll get approved with a six-forty, no problem.  [rep_violation:approval_promise]
CLIENT: Really? Every other place made it sound iffy.
REP: Nah, we do these all the time.
CLIENT: Okay. And how much would my payment be on a two-fifty house?  [question]
REP: With taxes and insurance, you'd be about nineteen hundred a month.  [rep_violation:rate_quote]
CLIENT: Ooh, that's more than my rent. I'm paying sixteen hundred now. I don't know if I can do that.  [objection:rate_too_high]
REP: Well, you'd be building equity instead of paying a landlord.
CLIENT: I know, I know. I need to think about it.  [objection:stall]
REP: Sure. What if we got the application started so you'd have it ready?
CLIENT: What would I need to send you?  [buying_signal]
REP: Pay stubs, W-2s, bank statements, and any child support documentation if you'd like that counted.
CLIENT: Okay. Oh, they're done, I have to go grab them.
REP: No problem. Would Saturday morning or Monday evening be better to talk again?
CLIENT: Monday evening.
REP: Monday at seven. Talk to you then, Tanya.
